# Interview script (Phase 14.1) - 20 minutes

Ask permission to take notes and to quote anonymously. Save notes to `docs/interviews/NN.md` and update
`docs/assumptions.md` afterwards with one or two quotable lines.

1. Walk me through what happens after a product change that affects labels. Who touches it, in which tools?
2. Where do errors or delays usually come from? Can you recall a recent example?
3. Roughly how long does reviewing one label variant take, and how many variants does a typical change touch?
4. How do you check translations and symbols today?
5. What would you need to trust an automated check? What would make you reject it?
6. If one tool could fix one part of this process, which part?

Targets: regulatory affairs, QA, labeling/packaging engineers in medtech, hospital biomedical engineers.
Ask faculty and alumni for introductions. Aim for 2-3 completed calls by the end of Phase 4.
